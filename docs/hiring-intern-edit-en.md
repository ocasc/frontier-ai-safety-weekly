# Editorial Intern, Frontier AI Safety Biweekly (remote, internship certificate provided)

## About us

Open Community for AI Safety China (OCASC) is a Chinese-language AI safety community of researchers, engineers, and people from policy and law. The community runs courses, paper reading groups, seminars and meetups, and has around 600 members.

Frontier AI Safety Biweekly comes out every two weeks: nine pieces published as one long visual article on WeChat, with a ten-minute podcast made from the same material. Sources are arXiv papers, model and system cards released by labs, and evaluation reports. The first draft is generated by our pipeline and finished by an editor.

## The work

Editing. The drafts are written by scripts and models; you edit them until they can go out.

- Check the figures and conclusions against the paper. If a source can't be traced, the claim comes out.
- Rewrite the Chinese where it reads like translation. Use the term readers know, and leave a term in English when no Chinese version has settled.
- Add background where a reader outside the field would otherwise be lost.
- Keep headlines within what the research shows, and make sure the article and the podcast agree.

From the third issue you can take an issue yourself, from picking the stories to publishing it.

## Requirements

- You write Chinese clearly.
- You can read a paper in English without a translator.
- 6 to 10 hours a week, for at least three months.
- You've written articles, reports or translations before, or worked in research.

Prior knowledge of AI safety isn't required.

## Practical details

Remote, with the option of meeting in Shanghai. The stipend is set by stage: the intern stage and the issue-lead stage are discussed separately. An internship certificate is provided.

- Six or seven issues in three months, twenty-six in a year if you stay
- Your work published publicly, and usable in a portfolio
- A read-through and written comments from the lead editor before every issue
- A reference for those who stay the course
- Community courses, seminars and meetups, open to the team

## How to apply

Fill in the [application form](https://wj.qq.com/s2/28521902/2n7h) (in Chinese, about two minutes). Attach one or two samples of your Chinese writing on technology, AI or policy, plus a LinkedIn, personal site or CV link.

We reply within one week. If it looks like a good fit, we send you a real card to edit and we both see how it goes.

Rolling review until the positions are filled.
