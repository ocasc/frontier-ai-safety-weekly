# Editorial Intern, Frontier AI Safety Biweekly (remote, internship certificate provided)

## About us

Open Community for AI Safety China (OCASC) is a Chinese-language AI safety community of researchers, engineers, and people from policy and law. The community runs courses, paper reading groups, seminars and meetups, and has around 600 members.

Frontier AI Safety Biweekly is the community's publication: nine pieces every two weeks, published as one long visual article on WeChat with a companion podcast. Sources are arXiv papers, model and system cards released by labs, and evaluation reports. Drafts are generated by our pipeline and finalised by an editor before release.

## Responsibilities

1. Copy-edit the draft: verify figures, conclusions and sources; rewrite machine-translated phrasing into readable Chinese; keep terminology consistent
2. Add background for readers outside the field, within a fixed length
3. Check that headlines match the findings, and that the visual article and the podcast script agree
4. Support layout and publication, and sign off before an issue goes out
5. From the third issue onward, take responsibility for an issue's topic selection and editing

## Requirements

1. Strong written Chinese, able to edit and proofread independently
2. Able to read papers and reports in English without translation tools
3. 6 to 10 hours a week, for at least 3 months
4. Published writing, reports or translations preferred; a research background is also acceptable
5. Prior knowledge of AI safety is not required

## What the internship offers

1. Six or seven issues edited over three months
2. Work published publicly and usable in a portfolio
3. A lead editor reviews each issue and gives written feedback
4. A stipend, set by stage
5. An internship certificate; strong performers receive a reference
6. Access to community courses, seminars and meetups

## How to apply

Fill in the [application form](https://wj.qq.com/s2/28521902/2n7h) (about two minutes). Attach one or two samples of Chinese writing on technology, AI or policy, plus a LinkedIn, personal site or CV link.

We reply within one week, and invite shortlisted candidates to a trial edit. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis.
