# Notice: third-party content

Issues produced with this toolkit quote figures, diagrams, and screenshots
from third-party sources (research papers, blogs, system cards, incident
reports) for commentary, with attribution in the figure caption ("图：…（来源）").

- Such quoted material **remains the property of its original owners** and is
  **not** covered by this repository's MIT or CC BY 4.0 licenses.
- Redistribution of a rendered issue reproduces that quoted material; if you
  republish issues, you are responsible for the quotation's attribution and
  for complying with the original sources' terms.
- The editorial policy is to quote original figures and screenshots directly
  and never redraw them (see `docs/content-guidelines.md`); clearly labelled
  translations ("据原图中译") are derived from the original figure and remain
  subject to the original owner's rights.
